For urgent gate-day issues, call the support line and keep your order reference ready.

Your QR ticket is sent to the phone number or email used at checkout. Search for the event name or your Twende Events order reference if it is not immediately visible.

Send the M-Pesa confirmation code, amount paid, payment time, event name, and checkout phone number to support@twendeevents.co.ke.

Contact support with the order reference and the new attendee details. Some events restrict transfers close to gate opening, so send the request early.

Email the team with your event date, venue, artwork, expected attendance, ticket tiers, and preferred launch date.

Email support@twendeevents.co.ke from the address on your account and ask for it to be deleted. We'll confirm your identity, then permanently remove your account and personal data, keeping only what we're legally required to retain (e.g. payment records for past orders).
